People

Manage who can talk to Forge and what they can do

What can each role do?
Commander

Full control. Forge answers everything and takes any action. Replies go directly.

Staff

Can ask about TB projects, schedule, general questions. Can create notes, reminders, and action items. Cannot access financials, send emails, or post to groups. Replies go directly.

Client

Can only ask about their own project status. Cannot take any actions. All replies are drafted for your approval first.

Vendor

Can ask about PO status, payments, and deliveries. Cannot take any actions. All replies are drafted for your approval first.

Personal

Can ask general questions and about schedule. Can create reminders. All replies are drafted for your approval first.
